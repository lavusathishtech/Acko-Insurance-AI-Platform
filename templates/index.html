Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to standard vehicle and health policy queries.

NCB is a progressive discount (ranging from 20% to 50%) applied to the Own Damage premium portion if you complete consecutive policy years without filing a claim.

Insured Declared Value (IDV) is the maximum sum assured that the insurer pays if the vehicle suffers total loss or theft. It represents the manufacturer's listed selling price adjusted for age-related depreciation.
